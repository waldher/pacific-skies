# P-38 Lightning model

Generated by `node tools/build-aircraft.mjs`, using the same local GLB pipeline as the Corsair and Zero.

The model has a 15.8496 m wingspan (52 ft) and 11.5316 m overall length (37 ft 10 in), matching the [National Museum of the United States Air Force's P-38L dimensions](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/article/196280/lockheed-p-38l-lightning/). This is an artistic arcade representation: internal stations, nacelles, canopy, and surface detail are approximate. Its flight silhouette includes the central cockpit pod, paired engine booms, twin vertical tails, connecting tailplane, and two three-blade propellers. Gear is retracted; the model height therefore should not be compared to the museum's height on landing gear.

`Airframe`, `Propeller`, and `Propeller_2` are separate asset nodes. The runtime batches static meshes by material and independently animates the propellers in opposite directions. Like the other game aircraft, the render wingspan is normalized to the game's shared aircraft size.
