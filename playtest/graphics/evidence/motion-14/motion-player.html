Advancing simulation evidence

Three seconds of actual gameplay updates for fleet combat and coastal flight, each at both phone profiles. Coastal flight uses the normal player-follow camera; shoreline visibility is recorded for every sample. Sparse sampled frames, not a 60 FPS recording. Playback interpolates no missing frames. Timing includes simulation, HUD submission, renderer and WebGL completion. Deferred DOM/layout/Canvas2D presentation and compositor costs are excluded; this does not measure device FPS.
