# Blind HUD judge C

Compared two five-scene sets across both phone render profiles. Grouped by visible HUD construction. Benchmarks inform principles separately from the A/B calculation.

- First glance, rounded (view-39, view-02, view-45): pale brackets select the centered aircraft; upper left is Hull and Gun heat; the two high-center ribbons are active outpost and base navigation. Both colored world marks compete as candidate destinations in the fleet. Bomb count, Fire and Map are evident. On the bow the bracket location is faster to find than the aircraft body.
- First glance, angular (view-03, view-19, view-21, view-33): light/dark squares still pick the center aircraft, the little gold strokes to the right suggest the outpost bearing, the dark top card explicitly names the active course, and the small green line gives distance/time to a secondary base. I read the top-left gauge for hull and heat; the bracket edges themselves do not reveal status at the supplied state. The runway paint arrow, chart heading and double-chevron are different marks, though the two gold strokes read like a local waypoint before their precise distance or relationship is clear. FIRE/HOLD RIGHT is actionable as text, and Bomb 2/2 and Map remain easy to find. Compact map and heat labels are still secondary-reading size.
- The angular treatment improves hierarchy with one header, one strong destination and one subordinate base line, and its flat rules and square plates feel more like aircraft instruments. Its dark top-center block also occludes upper fleet action more completely than the translucent pair. At the hangar the player sits across the roof edge; at the crowded bow a cream wing and sparks can still consume the dark player's body. The bracket survives these overlaps more reliably than the wings.
- Dead Space integration **falls short**. The visible luminous segments on Isaac’s spine remain one connected shape with the subject. Pacific Skies' squares identify the pilot locally, but I still need the corner readout to infer condition in these healthy-state stills.
- Ghost of Tsushima restraint **falls short overall**. The shrine still clears the display, and the combat still places compact contextual palettes in lower corners. The angular HUD also keeps the center open between the subject and fire control and subordinates the reserve cue, but keeps a large mission plate, map and weapon buttons visible in both quiet coastal traversal and the busiest fleet still. The debug wind frame explains the world cue mechanism without showing production motion. Gold chevrons here are compact directional symbols rather than environmental guidance demonstrated in these stills.
- No confirmed blocker in supplied stills. Urgent damage and overheat, objective arrival/capture changes, hitbox and safe-area behavior, and simultaneous two-thumb operation remain unverified. The bright runway, dark sea and visually busy carrier do appear in both render profiles.

Sampled progression addendum. Both scenes show damaged Hull 35% to 27%, a short hull bar, a white hit flash and separate heat fill. Angular pilot-bracket color fragments register locally on close inspection, but the legible damage readout remains the fixed gauge. The coast changes to ON STATION and adds RECOVERY near the runway. The fleet stays ACTIVE COURSE on RETURN TO BASE. Overheat endpoint, actual recovery completion and touch remain open. See the separate motion notes for recovery locator occlusion.
