## Benchmark comparison

- **Dead Space integration still falls short.** The local HULL label gives a precise low-health value as part of finding the plane and stays on it through the sampled bank and hit. The Dead Space light itself is physically built into the spine and semantically reads as a meter. Pacific Skies still needs a floating word-and-number label, and I cannot read gun lockout on the aircraft.
- **Ghost of Tsushima guidance and restraint still fall short.** The extended bearing mark separates from selection, and secondary text recedes in the busy urgent shot. Sony's exploration capture lets a visible landmark do most of the work and its effects still shows directional particles emerging from the world. Pacific Skies still leans on a fixed dark order panel plus chart. The Sony combat shot also leaves the upper frame clear while Pacific Skies occupies it persistently. Outpost capture and full touch behavior are outside this evidence.
