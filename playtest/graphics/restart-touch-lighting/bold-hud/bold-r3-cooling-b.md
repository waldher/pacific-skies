My first-glance read is now “the guns are temporarily unavailable and cooling.” GUNS HOT / COOLING takes the place of FIRE / HOLD RIGHT directly where the player would try to fire, and GUNS COOLING next to the top bar independently confirms the state. The outlined control and text give redundant cues without relying on a warm color. The prior heated shot told the player to hold the control and only signaled heat with a fill bar and tint.

Both cooling readouts are legible at the four captured sizes. The 27% hull warning stays on the aircraft and in the corner. Course, chart and Bomb 2/2 retain their positions and hierarchy. The extra line in the upper-left status panel fits even in short landscape and portrait, with no visible overlap or added panel. I saw no material regression in these pairs. The reported browser lockout and recovery exercise closes the visual ambiguity on gun availability; ergonomic use on hardware remains outside this test.

The benchmark calls hold. Dead Space's RIG still puts status on the suit, while the player in Pacific Skies looks to a remote control or panel for heat. Ghost of Tsushima still uses the scene itself to carry guidance and leaves much of the screen open. The improved urgent wording addresses action legibility on phone, an area the console stills do not test.
