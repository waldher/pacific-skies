# Combined candidate verdict

The three blind visual means are 7.575 / 7.665 / 7.700 against paired touch-lighting means of 7.399 / 7.2625 / 7.400. Median gain +0.300 with no scene's majority readability regression. Reference means 8.720 / 8.460 / 8.535 remain higher. Each reviewer viewed 45 stills and 36 sampled motion frames. Sampled motion passed. Continuous playback and listening gates remain open.

Three sequential matched pairs with 180 samples per scene used the same SwiftShader harness, browser and camera. The median p95 exceeds the original baseline plus 5% in eight of ten scenes. Fallback fleet 5.6 to 10.5 ms, fallback dogfight 10.9 to 13.5 ms, native stress 25.4 to 27.3 ms. This is a failed performance gate, not a device FPS measurement. The combined source remains an experimental visual lead. No deploy or merge.

The fleet slab patch applies to the combined runtime. The frame timing patch applies to the PCM-enabled capture tool only. They are follow-up trials, not accepted changes. The exact capture reports are preserved here; their image and media bytes are local.
