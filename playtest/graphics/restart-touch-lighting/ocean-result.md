# Fine-ripple trials

All three reviewers individually inspected the 45 stills and 36 sampled motion frames per trial. Weighted visual means (A/B/C) for touch lighting were 7.416 / 7.263 / 7.400, for lossy ripples 7.351 / 7.355 / 7.315, and for lossless ripples 7.419 / 7.475 / 7.415. Median gain over the lossy trial was 0.100, below the unchanged 0.15 gate. Frozen reference means were 8.720 / 8.460 / 8.535. Neither ocean trial is accepted.

The lossless asset removed the visible compression grid at the same decoded texture size. Single SwiftShader captures do not pass the matched performance gate. Sampled motion passed with wake and HUD caveats. Full functional suite for touch lighting passed. Exact runtime texture bytes are included in the binary patches. PNGs and original generator source are not in this checkpoint. Candidate 17 remains the preview build.
