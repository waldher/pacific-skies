# Pilot chevron HUD follow-up

The detached cyan chevron preserves player identification in the sampled bank. It sits just below the central gray fighter, aligned with its fuselage, and leaves the wings and silhouette unobstructed. The old cyan ring is easier to spot at a glance. The chevron takes a little longer to find because it is smaller and sits beside two bright tracers.

The cyan Hawk label helps separate the other aircraft from the pilot, but the small gold circle overlays Hawk in the chevron still and competes with its silhouette. The small gold direction pointer to the pilot's upper left is easy to miss on the water.

The two A/V stills come from separate live runs, with different positions for other aircraft and cues. This is a visual identification check at those moments. It does not establish tracking during motion or identical simulation state.
