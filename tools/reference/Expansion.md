# Expansion aircraft

Overall dimensions are in metres. Stations, cross-sections and details are
artistic approximations for game readability, not engineering measurements.
Aircraft share the gameplay wingspan normalization used by the existing loader.

| Model | Length | Span | Distinguishing geometry |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| SBD Dauntless | 9.9568 | 12.6492 | Radial cowl, long framed two-seat canopy, straight tapered wings, red perforated dive brakes, rear guns |
| TBF Avenger | 12.4714 | 16.51 | Deep weapons bay, broad wings, framed canopy, dorsal ball turret, tall broad tail |
| P-51D Mustang | 9.8298 | 11.2776 | Slim inline-engine nose, exhaust stacks, four-blade propeller, bubble canopy, belly radiator |

Dimensions from museum aircraft fact sheets:

- [Naval Aviation Museum SBD](https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/museums/nnam/explore/collections/aircraft/s/sbd-dauntless--sunken-treasure-.html): 32 ft 8 in long, 41 ft 6 in span.
- [Naval Aviation Museum Avenger](https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/museums/nnam/explore/collections/aircraft/t/tbm-avenger.html): 40 ft 11 in long, 54 ft 2 in span. The TBM is the General Motors-built Avenger; used for overall family proportions here.
- [Military Aviation Museum P-51D](https://www.militaryaviationmuseum.org/aircraft/north-american-p-51d-mustang/): 32 ft 3 in long, 37 ft span.

The closed dive-brake perforations use dark circular surface details, avoiding
expensive cut geometry at flight scale. Weapons and propulsion are arcade
approximations. These are original procedural assets, not museum mesh downloads.
